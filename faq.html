Got Questions?

Straight answers about joining, dues, and how things work in TALSUUDUS.

Every Tangaza student of Sokoto State indigene studying at Usmanu Danfodiyo University Sokoto becomes an automatic member of the union upon payment of the registration fee.

Newly registering students pay a one-time registration fee of ₦300. Members who are already registered pay ₦200 annually to renew. Of the ₦200, ₦100 goes toward the union I.D. card issued to fresh students.

Yes. Graduated members who are Sokoto State indigenes remain alumni members of the union, wherever they are, within Nigeria or abroad.

Open any member's profile in the Members Directory and use the "Report incorrect info" link, or reach out to an executive directly. New members should complete the official registration/consent process.

Elections and nominations for executive positions are conducted according to the union constitution. The Online Nomination Portal (currently under development) will let members submit nominations directly once it launches.

The Study Vault has a free, searchable library of past questions and notes contributed by members. You can also upload your own materials to help others.

Check the Announcements page, or join the official TALSUUDUS WhatsApp community using the green button on the site — that's where updates are usually shared first.

Reach out to any of the executive officers listed on the Leadership page, or message the union through the WhatsApp community.
